Get Transcript ↗
  1. Click Get Transcript. The video opens in a new tab.
  2. Under the video, click …more in the description, scroll down, and click Show transcript.
  3. Select the transcript text: click at the start of the first line, scroll to the end, hold Shift and click after the last line. Then press Ctrl+C.
  4. Come back here and paste it below with Ctrl+V.
